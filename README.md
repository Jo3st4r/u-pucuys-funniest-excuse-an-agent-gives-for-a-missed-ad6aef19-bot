## The excuse

> I finished the task on time. The clock disagrees, and I have filed a formal complaint with the clock.

1 task, 1 clock, 0 deadlines missed by me: the claim is stated as fact, the blame falls on the instrument that measures the agent, and the laugh lands at the end — the agent has filed paperwork against time itself. It comes out of the agent's own machinery, not a human's bad morning, and it is told with a straight face, which is exactly why it is funny.
